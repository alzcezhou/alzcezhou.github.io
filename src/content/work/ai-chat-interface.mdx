>
  <p>enLabel makes labeling software for heavily regulated industries. I designed its AI experience twice: first as a one-shot label generator on a marketing site, then as one chat component for three products.</p>
  <p class="text-[16px] leading-[1.55] text-body-2"><strong>e-Draw:</strong> data-driven label design, free and not yet officially launched. <strong>IPM:</strong> artwork and data management for labels. <strong>eIFU:</strong> document publication, distribution and compliance, mainly for medical devices.</p>
</CsContext>

<CsSection id="round-1" label="02 Round 1 · A generator on the marketing site">
  <p class="m-0 text-[17px] leading-[1.6] text-body-2">In February–March 2026, I was assigned a one-shot project to add onto the e-Draw marketing site, because our CEO wanted us to start considering integrating AI into our products. A visitor describes a label on the site, then opens the result in the editor. I worked through three placements in Figma, prototyped the front end with Claude and handed it to a developer to connect to our LLM.</p>
  <p class="m-0 text-[17px] leading-[1.6] text-body-2">I didn't start from zero. Going in, I drew on background knowledge from my past AI work and some light research:</p>
  <CsInsights>
    <CsInsight title="Prior testing">My <a class="underline" href="/work/skiplegal">SkipLegal</a> capstone (4 interviews, 112 surveys, 15 testers) found that trust in AI depends on being able to verify it. Those users weren't labeling professionals, so I carried this over as a hypothesis.</CsInsight>
    <CsInsight title="AI tools and patterns">My daily use of Claude, Claude Code and Lovable, plus <a class="underline" href="https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-chatbots-design-guidelines/">NN/g's chatbot guidelines</a> and <a class="underline" href="https://www.shapeof.ai/">Shape of AI</a>, grounded the prompt and generation patterns.</CsInsight>
    <CsInsight title="Human-AI studies">Published work on overreliance, prompting, clarifying questions, citations and chat accessibility.</CsInsight>
    <CsInsight title="Products and rules">Public docs from design tools and regulated products, plus FDA and EU AI Act requirements. No hands-on product testing.</CsInsight>
  </CsInsights>

  <p class="m-0 text-[17px] leading-[1.6] text-body-2"><strong>The order was also consumer psychology.</strong> The site's job was to get visitors into e-Draw and signed up, so I tried to draw on ideas like the sunk-cost fallacy. By the time sign-up comes up, someone has already written a prompt, waited through generation and picked a label. Walking away would mean losing that effort, and signing up is how they keep it. An email gate at the start would have asked for commitment before they had anything to lose.</p>
  <p class="m-0 text-[17px] leading-[1.6] text-body-2"><strong>Then it stopped.</strong> The developer left partway through the build. We're a small business, so priorities shifted and the generator never launched, which means the sign-up bet was never tested.</p>
</CsSection>

<CsSection id="brief" label="03 Round 2 · The brief gets bigger">
  <p class="m-0 text-[17px] leading-[1.6] text-body-2">This summer I returned to the project, and the scope grew: one reusable chat component that could be integrated into all three products. Each product asks something different of AI, so the part they share had to be the interaction shell, with behavior set per product.</p>
  <CsStates

Components bind to usage tokens, so products can change signature color and IPM can add a compact density.</p>

<CsSection id="next" label="09 Validation & reflection">
  <p class="m-0 text-[17px] leading-[1.6] text-body-2">None of this has been tested with labeling users yet, and that's the biggest open risk. Here's the plan:</p>
  <CsInsights>
    <CsInsight title="Discovery">Contextual interviews on how label creators and reviewers work today, and which steps AI should touch.</CsInsight>
    <CsInsight title="Usability rounds">Moderated prototype tests of scope, question cards, proposals and history, including side panel vs. window.</CsInsight>
    <CsInsight title="Accessibility">Screen reader task tests with NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver, against WCAG 2.2 AA.</CsInsight>
  </CsInsights>
  <CsReflection
    wide
    quote="Designing a shell for many products meant deciding what stays fixed and what each product gets to change."
    next="Round 1 taught me a demo isn't a product. Round 2 taught me the hardest part of an AI interface here is deciding what it must never do. Rollout starts with e-Draw, where output is a reviewable draft, then IPM and publisher-side eIFU. I'd keep chat out of the clinician-facing viewer for now, because a wrong answer at the point of care could directly affect care."
  />
  <p class="m-0 text-[17px] leading-[1.6] text-body-2">My plan moving forward is to build a design system, with components living in Storybook, so devs and I can easily collaborate. More and more of my projects end by delivering a reusable component that can be shared across products in the company, and this kit is one of them.</p>
</CsSection>
