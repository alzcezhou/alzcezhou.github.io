<CsContext
  id="context"
  label="01 Context"
  src="/images/skiplegal/immigration%20flowchart.png"
  alt="Immigration process flowchart"
>
  <p>SkipLegal is a startup using AI to guide people through U.S. visa, green card, and citizenship applications.</p>
  <p>For our capstone, we designed its core workflow from scratch: securely uploading sensitive immigration documents, with AI that autofills forms in real time and a chatbot for support.</p>
  <p>The catch: one typo can cost months and thousands of dollars. Would users trust AI with that?</p>
</CsContext>

<CsSection id="research" label="02 Research · 4 interviews · 112 surveys · 15 testers">
  <CsInsights>
    <CsInsight title="Overwhelmed">The process is unclear and repetitive, even with a lawyer.</CsInsight>
    <CsInsight title="Show your work">Trust in AI depends on being able to verify it.</CsInsight>
    <CsInsight title="Prove it's safe">Without a clear privacy explanation, users won't upload.</CsInsight>
  </CsInsights>
</CsSection>

    <CsDecision
      title="Eligibility quiz first"
      problem="terms like “petitioner” made testers hesitate."
      decision="one question per screen + inline definitions; answers tailor the workspace."
      outcome="smoother onboarding in round 2."
    />
    <CsDecision
      title="Autofill or Manual"
      problem="testers couldn't tell if the AI fills the form or just answers questions."
      decision="explicit mode toggle; AI explains what it fills first."
      outcome="<strong>+37% trust rating.</strong>"
    />
  </CsDecisions>
</CsSection>
