<CsContext
  id="context"
  label="01 Context"
  src="/images/haven/case-study/cover.png"
  alt="Haven mobile screens: map and incident reporting"
>
  <p>Victims often avoid reporting incidents to both local authorities and their institutions due to stigma and fear. Victims who do report often do not get the justice they deserve.</p>
  <p>What if reporting incidents could be entirely anonymous and crowd-sourced? What if communities were empowered to keep themselves safe?</p>
</CsContext>

  <CsDecisions>
    <CsDecision
      title="Anonymity from the first screen"
      images={[
        { src: "/images/haven/case-study/onboarding-1.png", alt: "Onboarding: create account with university email" },
        { src: "/images/haven/case-study/onboarding-2.png", alt: "Onboarding: welcome screen" },
      ]}
      problem="fear of being identified was the #1 barrier."
      decision="verify with a university email; collect nothing identifying."
      outcome="trust is set before any sensitive content is asked for."
    />
    <CsDecision
      title="Crowdsourced trust signals"
      images={[
        { src: "/images/haven/case-study/resource-finder.png", alt: "Resource map with provider cards" },

      ]}
      problem="institutional directories lack peer trust."
      decision="map-based resources with crowdsourced ratings and services at a glance."
      outcome="students judge a provider's safety fast, in high-stress moments."
